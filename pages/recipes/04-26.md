## 04/13

### blueberry muffins

have used [this recipe][bb-muff-recipe] for a while and had p good results

#### ingredients
- 2 cups flour
- 1 cup granulated sugar
- 2 tsps baking powder
- 2 eggs
- 1 stick of butter
- 1/2 cup milk
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 pint blueberries

#### preparation
- melt butter in medium bowl and let cool
- preheat oven to 400
- spray muffin pan/add liners
- in large bowl, add flour, sugar, & baking powder
- in bowl w/ melted butter add eggs, milk, vanilla extract
- combine wet and dry into lumpy dough
- fold in washed blueberries
- add to muffin tins
- bake for 20 minutes

#### final result

makes 12 [blueberry muffins][bb-muff-img]

## 04/14

### chickpea pasta salad

just recreating a pasta salad my mom would make in spring, but with protein!

#### ingredients
- 1 cucumber
- 1 bell pepper
- 8 oz mozzarella pearls
- 10 oz cherry tomatoes
- 16 oz shell pasta
- 16 oz can of chickpeas
- 1 oz olive oil
- 1 tbsp lemon juice

#### preparation
- set 4 qts of salter water to boil
- in the meantime:
	- slice cucumber into slices and quarter
	- slice strips of bell pepper
	- split mozzarella into individual pearls
	- wash cherry tomatoes
- once water is boiling, reduce heat
- boil pasta to cook for 8-10 minutes
- strain pasta and let cool in pot
- rinse chickpeas in same strainer and add to pot
- add veggies to pot
- add dash of olive oil and lemon juice

#### final result

- [serving size][chick-psta-sld-img]
- makes 6 servings
